Prediction and Analysis of Amazon sales Metrics Using Machine Learning

The goal of this project is to analyze and predict cut off related to amazon sale(on behalf of discount ), using machine learning models on real-world structured data.

Conclusion =>The model was able to predict cut-offs with decent accuracy. =>The model achieve good accuracy and showed that price,discounts,product category,and customer region. =>The important factor effective sales.

Column Name And Description order id :--- Unique identifier for each order

order_date :--- date of the order

product_id:---number code of a product

product_category :--- category of differnt product(e.g.,Books,Fashion,Sport,Beauty,Electronic,Home & Kitchen)

price :--- purchasing price of product

discount_percent :--- discount percent for a product

quantity_sold :--- sold quantity of a product

customer_region :--- sold good at different customer region(country)

payment_method :--- good puchased by different way(UPI/Credit Card/Debit Card)

Rating :--- rating by the customer on behalf of product

review_count:--- count the no of review

discounted_price:--- company sold to ustmer on bhehalf of disount percent

total_revenue :--- tottal of revenue of a poduct
